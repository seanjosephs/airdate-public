airdate

    welcome to airdate

    airdate is a catalog for the essays in your Obsidian vault.

    It walks each one from first draft to ready, and gives it a day on your calendar.

    It creates Substack drafts. It never publishes, schedules or emails.

    how airdate talks to Substack

    Substack has no official API for drafts, so airdate uses an unofficial client. Your Substack session stays inside Obsidian, in the airdate connector; airdate never sees your password. SETUP.md has the details.

    your vault

    airdate reads essays from a folder in your Obsidian vault.

    The vault folder is set in config.json on the machine running airdate.

    your publication

    Where your drafts go. You can fill this in later; sending stays blocked until it is set.

    how you organize

    Do you keep essays in topic folders?

    your totems

    Five marks for telling your kinds of work apart at a glance. Rename them, pick a color, and swap the icon for any image in your vault.

    your tags

    Optional. A tag preset is a name, a color and up to five tags you reach for often. The editor adds them in one click. Skip this and add them later in settings.

    your calendar

    Pick the day you publish. The calendar gives you one slot per week on that day.

    connect Substack

    Three steps in Obsidian. You can do this later; sending stays blocked until it is done.
