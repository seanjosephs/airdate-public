        airdate
        
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          welcome to airdate
          
        
          airdate is a writers room for the essays in your obsidian vault.
          
        
          it walks each one from first draft to ready, and gives it a day on your board.
          
        
          it creates substack drafts. it never publishes, schedules or emails.
          
        
          
          how airdate talks to substack
          
          substack has no official api for drafts, so airdate uses an unofficial client. your substack session stays inside obsidian, in the airdate connector; airdate never sees your password. SETUP.md has the details.
          
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          your vault
          
        
          airdate reads essays from a folder in your obsidian vault.
          
        
        
          the vault folder is set in config.json on the machine running airdate.
